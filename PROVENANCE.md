# Provenance

MatchFlow is a continuation of entity matching research originating in the
[anhaidgroup](https://github.com/anhaidgroup) (Magellan Data Management Group)
at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

## Lineage

MatchFlow builds on a sequence of prior matching systems developed within the
Magellan group:

- **ActiveMatcher** — earlier active-learning-based matching system
- **MadLib** — refactored and extended internal predecessor
- **MatchFlow** — current ML-based matching with active learning, evolved from the above

The lineage from ActiveMatcher and MadLib means MatchFlow's IP ownership and
copyright status are consistent with other anhaidgroup-developed entity matching
systems (Sparkly, Delex), regardless of where the active development repository
physically lives.

## Repository History

This repository was created in the MadMatcher organization on GitHub during the
period when MadMatcher was emerging from the Magellan group. The administrative
placement of the repository does not affect the underlying IP status of the code.
